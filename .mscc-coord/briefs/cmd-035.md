## Problem

On Win11 **local** host+client Digital, WSJT TUNE/TX shows a brief wide SA hash bump at key-up, then a clean tone. Pi local does not. Likely stale PortAudio/VAC mic samples (or mute timing) hitting the modulator the instant `CMD_SET_TX_ON` unmutes.

Stew wants a narrow **local clear + short mute gate on TX 0->1** first. Remote Win->Pi skirts are a later observation only.

### Recommended hook points (hypothesis)

1. **`udp_thread.c` `case CMD_SET_TX_ON:`** on `previous_G_tx_mode != G_tx_mode` and `G_tx_mode == 1`, **only if** `G_audio_mode` is `DIGITAL_AUDIO` or `OPERATOR_AUDIO`:
   - Arm a short **TX key-up mute gate** (~30-40 ms of silence into TX).
   - Prefer: keep mic effectively silent (volume 0 or callback zeros) for the gate, **then** call existing `Set_Mic_Mute(FALSE)`.
   - Do **not** apply this gate for remote-only modes 2/3.
2. **`main.c` `sdrAudioCallback`** (and/or just before `framesToComplex`): while gate active, force **silence into the modulator** (zero samples / skip modulating mic). Prefer silence / discard stale into TX - **not** hold-last. Counting down by `framesPerBuffer` (or sample count @ 96 kHz) is fine.
3. Optional tiny helpers in `udp_thread.c` / `main.c` + `extern.h` (e.g. `G_tx_mic_gate_frames`) - keep narrow.

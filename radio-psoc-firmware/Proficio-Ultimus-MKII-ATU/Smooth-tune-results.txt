Analysis (2026-07-22) — why residual pops remain
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
v1 still recomputed Multisynth every step as even(900 MHz / fout). That
creates hard MS boundaries inside bands:

  Classic ceiling method: fout_clk = LO*4, divider = even(900e6/fout)
  LO-class edge near 900e6/(16)/4 = 14.0625 MHz  (~your 14.080 with offsets)
  LO-class edge near 900e6/(8)/4  = 28.125 MHz   (~your 28.140 with offsets)

On LF the *same* even-divider steps are dense:
  ~80–170 Hz LO spacing on 2200 m  → matches "<100 Hz"
  ~1–2 kHz LO spacing on 630 m     → matches "1 kHz"

So LF residual was NOT primarily ppm-window size — it was Multisynth
changing every few hundred Hz and forcing PLL_RESET.
